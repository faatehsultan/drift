A whole toolkit hiding
in plain sight.

Move your pointer to the top of the screen and Drift springs open. Move away and it tucks itself back into the notch.

🎵

Music, with real lyrics

Search, play, queue and browse YouTube Music natively. Synced lyrics scroll with the song; tap a line to jump to it.

📥

Drop files on the notch

Drag anything to the top of your screen to park it. AirDrop it, merge PDFs, or convert images, video and docs in a click.

📅

Never miss a meeting

Your next event and a live countdown, with one-click Join for Zoom, Meet, Teams and Webex.

⏱️

Focus and get stuff done

Timers and Pomodoro that count down in the notch, quick notes, to-dos, your Shortcuts and a camera mirror.

✨

Live activities

Now playing, timers, meetings, charging and new files show up right in the notch, like the Dynamic Island.

Claude Code asks for permission in the notch. Allow or deny without leaving what you're doing.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

1

Download

Grab Drift.dmg and drag Drift into Applications.

2

Open it

The first time, macOS may block it. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

3

Hover the notch

That's it. Drift updates itself whenever a new version ships.
