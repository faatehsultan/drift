# Code of Conduct

Drift is a friendly project. We want everyone who uses or contributes to it to feel welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that people have different experience, opinions and ways of working.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Trolling, or repeatedly derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, code or issues that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile ([@faatehsultan](https://github.com/faatehsultan)). Reports are kept confidential.
